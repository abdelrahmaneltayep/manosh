## Full description

**Stop losing wholesale orders in your inbox.**

Wholesale buying still runs on email: a buyer asks for a price, you build a spreadsheet, they reply with changes, you re-quote, and days later maybe an order lands. Mannon collapses that whole loop into a single link.

**Build a quote in under a minute.**
Add products, set quantities, apply your wholesale pricing, and choose payment terms. Mannon prices the quote using Shopify's own tax and totals engine — so what your buyer sees is exactly what they'll be charged. No manual tax math, no surprises.

**Your buyers accept it themselves.**
Send the quote and your buyer opens a clean, branded portal through a secure magic link — no password, no login wall. They can accept, request a change, or reorder a past quote in one tap. Every quote is tracked from *sent → countered → accepted → ordered* so nothing gets lost.

**Native payment terms, on any plan.**
Offer net 7, 15, 30, 45, 60, or 90 days, or due-on-fulfillment — built on Shopify's native B2B, now available beyond Plus. Your books stay clean because every accepted quote becomes a real Shopify order.

**AI Magic Order Pad.**
Your buyer pastes a messy list — SKUs, product names, "12x the blue hoodie" — and Mannon turns it into a ready cart they confirm before anything happens. AI drafts; a human always confirms.

**Why merchants choose Mannon**
- Works on **Basic, Shopify, and Advanced** — not just Plus
- Quotes priced by Shopify, so tax and totals are always correct
- Passwordless buyer portal — zero friction, higher accept rates
- Every quote is a tracked, reorderable record
- Native net terms your finance team already understands
- Fast, embedded, and built to Shopify's App Store standards

**Try it free for 14 days.** No credit card to start.
