# B2B store leads for Mannon (66 candidates)

Compiled 2026-11-23 for the outreach in `docs/promotion-plan.md` (section 5.1).

**These are candidates, not confirmed leads.** Every store and URL below came from a
web search result seen during the research; none was added from memory. But the
research could not open any store's website, so two things are unconfirmed for every
row and must be checked in a browser before contact:

1. **That the store runs on Shopify.** The only evidence is URL shape: Shopify stores
   use `/pages/…`, `/collections/…` and `/products/…`. That is a strong hint, not proof.
2. **What the wholesale page says today.** The "Signal" column is a search-result title
   or the search tool's summary of the page, which can paraphrase or be out of date.

A spot-check of 6 stores by fresh searches found: 3 fully consistent (Labboho, Endro
Cosmétiques, Fasteners Plus); 1 where the store looks like Shopify but the wholesale
page did not come up again (Canada Candle Co.); and 2 with mixed platform signals, where
the same domain also shows non-Shopify URL shapes (Simple Lighting, St Martins Coffee).
Expect roughly a third of the list to drop out on verification.

## How to verify a row (about two minutes each)

1. Open the page URL. If it is gone or no longer about wholesale, mark `Poor fit`.
2. View the page source and search for `cdn.shopify.com`. Not there: mark
   `Not Shopify` and skip.
3. Read how buyers get prices: a form, an email address, an application, or a login
   portal with live prices. A self-serve portal is a `Poor fit`.
4. Note the public business contact route shown on the page (form, wholesale@ or
   sales@ address, company LinkedIn). Do not record personal names, personal emails or
   mobile numbers.
5. Note one specific, true detail to open the message with.

Whether the store uses Shopify's company accounts cannot be seen from outside. Ask in
the first message, as the template in the promotion plan does.

## Columns

- **Fit A:** asks buyers to email, apply or request a quote for pricing. Best fit.
- **Fit B:** has a wholesale or trade page but looks larger, or already runs a
  self-serve portal. Lower priority.
- **Check:** `pattern` = Shopify URL shapes seen in results; `unverified` = not enough
  URL evidence; `mixed` = non-Shopify URL shapes also seen on the domain.

## How the list was built

- Three research passes on 2026-11-23, each using web search only, about 30 searches
  each. The passes were told to add nothing from memory and to copy URLs exactly.
- Marketplaces, agencies, app vendors, directories and household-name enterprises were
  excluded. No personal data was collected.
- Searches that worked best, for finding more of the same:
  - one product word plus `"pages/wholesale"`, for example `candles "pages/wholesale" wholesale enquiry`
  - `"pages/request-a-quote"` or `"pages/bulk-orders"` plus a trade category
  - French: `"pages/devenir-revendeur" OR "pages/revendeur"` plus a product word
  - then a search limited to the store's own domain for `collections products`, to see
    Shopify's URL shapes

## Gaps to fill next

1. **Arabic-language stores.** Search engines surfaced none. Better routes: Shopify's
   own regional showcases, Arabic Instagram or LinkedIn business pages that link to a
   store, and the founder's own network.
2. **Country is unknown for 25 rows.** The store's footer or shipping page settles it.
3. **Size.** Nothing here measures order volume. The first reply usually reveals it.
