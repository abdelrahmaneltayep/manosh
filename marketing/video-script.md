# Mannon — demo reel script

One-line VO + on-screen action per beat. Keep it calm and concrete; no music
over dialogue.

## Hero beat — AI counter-offer (Feature 1)

> **VO:** "A buyer asks for a better price — Mannon suggests the counter, checks
> your margin, and drafts the reply, so you answer in one click instead of one
> afternoon."
>
> **On screen:** Inside an open quote, the merchant clicks **AI suggest** on a
> line. The suggestion panel opens: **suggested price**, a green **margin badge**,
> a short **rationale**, and a **drafted buyer message**. The merchant clicks
> **Accept (use this price)** — the counter field fills — then **Send counter**.
>
> _(This is the hero beat of the reel — lead with it.)_

## Supporting beats (existing pillars)

- **Passwordless portal.** VO: "Your buyer accepts from a secure link — no
  password, no login wall." On screen: the buyer opens the magic link and taps
  **Accept quote**.
- **Becomes a real order.** VO: "Every accepted quote becomes a real Shopify
  draft order — nothing lost in email." On screen: the draft order in Admin with
  matching totals.
- **AI Magic Order Pad.** VO: "Paste a messy list; Mannon drafts the cart — you
  confirm every line." On screen: pasted text → matched items → confirm.
- **One-tap reorder.** VO: "Past orders become one-tap reorder cards." On screen:
  a reorder card sent from the portal.
